Street photo key

To pull up a real street photo of a corner, you need a free key from Mapillary (a site where people share street-view photos). It takes about 2 minutes:

1. Go to mapillary.com/developer
2. Sign up / log in (it's free)
3. Click "Register application" — fill in any name, any URL
4. Copy the "Client Token" it gives you (starts with MLY|)
5. Paste it in the box below

This stays in your browser tab only — it's never saved or sent anywhere except to Mapillary itself.
