How the score works

Four steps, nothing hidden

1
Find the place

Your city name is turned into map coordinates using OpenStreetMap's free location lookup.

2
Search real news

We search a public, global news archive (GDELT) for recent articles near that place mentioning disasters or crime.

3
Check for live hazards

We cross-check against NASA's and the US Geological Survey's real-time disaster and earthquake feeds for anything confirmed and active nearby.

4
Add it up, out of 100

News volume and confirmed hazards combine into one score — low (green), moderate (blue), or high (red) — with every source listed underneath.

Disastorous is an independent hackathon project, not affiliated with any government or news agency.
News search via GDELT · Natural events via NASA EONET · Earthquakes via USGS · Location lookup & maps via OpenStreetMap.
Scores are automated estimates, not verified facts. Always check the linked sources yourself.
